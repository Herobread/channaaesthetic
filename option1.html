Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I committed to having a treatment during my consultation?

Not at all. Our consultations are designed to evaluate your skin and discuss options. You are under no obligation to proceed on the same day.

Who performs the injectables and advanced procedures?

All injectables and medical-grade treatments are carried out by qualified medical professionals with specialized training in facial anatomy and aesthetics.

Will my results look natural or obvious?

Our guiding philosophy is subtle enhancement. We use conservative dosing and precise placement to ensure your results look like a refreshed version of you, without an overdone or frozen look.

Is there any downtime after anti-wrinkle or filler treatments?

Most treatments have minimal to no downtime. You may experience slight redness or localized swelling at injection points, which typically subsides within 24–48 hours.
